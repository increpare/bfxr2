Bfxr desktop interface

Bfxr and its sound engines retain the repository's MIT license and original
attributions. See LICENSE and the About dialog.

The Windows 95 presentation is a new adaptation guided by the private Win95 UI
reference collection (14 October 2026): Vector95 and ProcGen FM. Those reference
applications are not bundled. Classic control colors and interaction patterns
are used here with Bfxr's existing controls.
