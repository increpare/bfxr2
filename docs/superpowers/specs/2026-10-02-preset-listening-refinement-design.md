# Refine Transfxr families from listening feedback

The user auditioned the catalogue and found that measured similarity did not reliably imply an audible species. Their feedback is the design input: keep Bass Plucks and Rubber Clicks, tighten short textured sounds, require actual sustained wavering, make Soft Pips genuinely gentle and distinguishable from fuzzy voices, separate sand from air, rename the low mournful voices and static fragments, and replace direction-only categories with timbres that have a stable identity. Reverse sounds need a much narrower range.

Use the 512-sound corpus as discovery material, not as a claim that every cluster member belongs in a shipped preset. Preserve the original discovery catalogue in an archive. The current catalogue should audition only revised, rendered family exemplars, and distinguish those from fresh generated variants. Every revised example retains its survey source ID and an explicit family profile describing editorial changes.

Ship 15 families: Bright Whistles, Grainy Taps, Rocket Zips, Wavering Calls, Fuzzy Chirps, Bass Plucks, Soft Pips, Sand Sprays, Air Currents, Mournful Calls, Arcade Zaps, Bubble Pops, Rubber Clicks, Bubble Swells and Radio Spits. Bass Plucks, Rubber Clicks and the renamed Mournful Calls retain their successful surveyed voices. The other banks are curated using meaningful duration, noise, envelope, waveform and trajectory constraints, not just new names.

Generic family sampling keeps whole exemplar correlations and small compatible interpolation. Family limits are applied inside sampling, before SynthBase applies parameter locks, so unlocked generation honors the character and locked controls remain user-controlled. Short dry categories remain dry. The catalogue's first six should be representative curated exemplars, with all curated variants also exposed. The raw 512-sound archive stays accessible for exploration.

Validation must check duration limits on complete rendered audio, not only the voice duration; actual sustained vibrato for Wavering Calls; tonal/noisy distinction between Soft Pips and Fuzzy Chirps; contrasting noise/envelope/filter profiles for sand and air; finite, audible, unclipped output; locks and legacy morph support. Use family-trait checks instead of a nearest-cluster percentage as the acceptance claim. User listening remains the aesthetic judge; do not claim an ear review that was not performed.
