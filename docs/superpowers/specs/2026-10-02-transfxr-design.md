# Finish Transfxr

The existing hidden synth contains only placeholder controls and no renderer. Finish its two-state concept as a continuous oscillator with independently curved pitch, low-pass cutoff, noise blend, vibrato depth and level. Use wide, directly editable graphs with orange A/B handles, numeric readouts and a strip of curve-shape buttons. Match Bfxr's 350px panel width. Drag a handle to change one endpoint, or the middle of a graph to move both while preserving their interval. Returning curves put B halfway through the graph. Arrow keys adjust focused handles, with Shift for fine adjustments. Graphs update throughout a drag; audio and persistence update on release. Duration, attack, release, resonance and echo shape the whole sound. Preserve the application's existing visual style and export/playback workflow.

A dedicated, DOM-free renderer produces deterministic mono 44.1 kHz PCM with continuous oscillator phase, band-limited saw/pulse oscillators, a stable resonant filter, short boundary fades and an echo tail. Transition curves stay bounded. Parameters use `{start, end, curve}` objects, copied and validated at the SynthBase boundary. Locks protect both endpoints and the curve during generation/mutation. Browser controls update serialized state even when autoplay is off.

Expose the Transfxr tab. Ship eight curated example recipes with small repeat-click variations, plus randomize and mutate. Also deliver a reloadable example collection and rendered WAV previews. Existing Bfxr and Footsteppr files and numeric share links remain compatible; collection import skips absent synth sections. Nested transitions survive file, collection and link round trips.

Validation: Node tests for transition state, lock behavior, safe curves, measurable pitch sweeps, deterministic finite audio, silent volume, bounded output, sample rate and exported content. Browser checks cover controls, curve selection, example generation, save/reload, legacy collection loading and export with autoplay disabled. Render examples for inspection. No external dependencies in the synth.
